Where do I find my section or team?

NMM: Open Western Student Center → Academics → Course Registration → Enrollment Summary. Find the NMM 2270A TUT component, not the lecture section. Match its section number or day/time to the selector above. Western’s instructions.

Monday sections: 008, 009, 011. Friday sections: 003, 004, 006, 012. Quiz dates are matched to the tutorial table in the supplied outline; follow any section-specific update from your TA.

Structures: Use your assigned CEE 2220A laboratory team number from OWL or your instructor. Choose the team range, then your exact team. This selects the lab session and report deadline; it does not change whole-class assessments.
